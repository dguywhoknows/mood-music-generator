Short pieces of music composed from a description of a mood.

Describe a mood or a scene and get a loop with chords, melody, bass and drums, played by a synthesizer in your browser. Change the tempo, sounds and mix, program your own drums, and export MIDI or WAV.

The app itself, running inside this page. Anything you do here is saved in your browser.

Ask it to do things

Press Ctrl+K in the app (Cmd+K on a Mac) and describe what you want. The model chooses from Mood Synth's own functions, runs them in order and tells you what changed. Some examples:

Rainy café at midnight, slow and jazzyComposes a new piece for that mood and plays it.
Slower, and swap the lead for something softerChanges the tempo and lead sound.
Save this to my libraryStores the piece with its mood.

Instructions you save in the same box, such as "keep it short" or "I'm a beginner", are added to every AI request the app makes.

Features

AI composition as JSON
Key, mode, tempo, 8-chord progression, 32-beat melody, drum style, timbres.
Chord-symbol parser
Maj7, m7b5, sus, add9, slash chords …) and scale/chord-tone quantizer.
Algorithmic composer fallback
Roman-numeral progressions per mode, chord-tone-anchored random-walk melodies.
Web Audio synth
ADSR voices, lowpass filtering, synthesized kick/snare/hats, generated reverb impulse, compressor.
Lookahead scheduler
Chris Wilson pattern) with humanized timing and velocity.
Canvas piano roll
With live playhead and chord highlighting.
Tempo
Transpose, timbre, drums, reverb controls; AI variations; Standard MIDI File export (4 tracks).
Library page
Save pieces with their mood, rename inline, pin favorites, sort by newest / most played / tempo, mini piano-roll previews, JSON export and import.
Chord lab page
Type any progression for Krumhansl–Schmuckler key detection with ranked candidates, roman-numeral analysis with harmonic function, cadence detection and borrowed-chord highlighting.
Reharmonization
Tritone substitutions, relative major/minor, secondary dominants and modal borrowing, plus AI reharmonization options; send a progression to the composer.

Runs in your browser

Music theory, quantization, synthesis, scheduling, visualization, MIDI writing. None of this needs a network connection or a model.

Uses a language model for

Mood → full structured composition. Variation mode: rewrites the melody as call-and-response while keeping the harmony.

Notes

Cost
Free. Each account has a daily allowance of AI requests.
Your data
What you make stays in this browser on this device. Your account is only used for signing in and for the built-in AI. You can export a backup or clear everything from Settings.
AI access
Signed-in users get the built-in AI with nothing to set up. You can also use your own Groq or OpenRouter key under Settings.
